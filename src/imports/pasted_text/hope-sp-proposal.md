Create a premium, executive-level **16:9 PowerPoint presentation design** for **eTranzact International Plc** to submit to the **Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction (FMHAPR)** for participation in the **HOPE-SP — Household Prosperity and Economic Social Protection Project**.

## CORE OBJECTIVE

The presentation should position eTranzact as a **Digital Disbursement Infrastructure Partner for HOPE-SP**, not merely as a payment processor.

The central narrative is:

**FROM APPROVED BENEFICIARY TO DELIVERED IMPACT**

eTranzact should be presented as the infrastructure that enables the Ministry to securely move programme funds from approved disbursement instructions to intended beneficiaries, while providing governance, authorization, visibility, reconciliation and scalable beneficiary access.

The proposal must feel like a serious submission to a Federal Government Ministry and should be suitable for presentation to the **Honourable Minister, Permanent Secretary, HOPE-SP Programme Leadership and senior government stakeholders.**

Do NOT mention competitors.

Do NOT position eTranzact as responsible for government beneficiary targeting or policy decisions. Clearly distinguish the Ministry's programme responsibility from eTranzact's technology and financial infrastructure role.

Create a sophisticated **government + fintech + institutional banking** visual identity.

Style:

* Trustworthy

* Modern Nigerian fintech
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Large numbers

Do not make the presentation look like a generic startup pitch deck.

Do not overuse gradients.

Use subtle Nigerian/public-sector visual cues where appropriate, but avoid cliché imagery.

* Editable text
